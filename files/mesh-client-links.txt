About
=====

One desktop app for the three major mesh ecosystems:

  * Meshtastic - LoRa mesh firmware (https://meshtastic.org/)
  * MeshCore   - companion radio mesh (https://www.meshcore.dev/)
  * Reticulum   - encrypted mesh stack (https://reticulum.network/)

Cross-platform Electron app for macOS, Linux, and Windows.
Connect over BLE, USB serial, or Wi-Fi/TCP. Local-first
message history is stored in SQLite.
